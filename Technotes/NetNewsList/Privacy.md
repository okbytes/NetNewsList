# NetNewsList privacy

NetNewsList has no servers and collects nothing.

- **Your reading list** (addresses, titles, extracted text, read and starred state) is stored on your devices and in your private iCloud database, which only your Apple ID can read. Apple’s terms for iCloud apply to it.
- **Images** from saved pages are stored on each device only, not in iCloud.
- **When you save a page**, the app downloads it, and later its images, from the site, as a browser would. Those sites see a normal request from your device.
- **X posts** are read from X's own page, loaded on your device. If you sign in to X in NetNewsList, your X session stays on that device (it isn't synced), X sees your account load each post you save, and Sign Out deletes the session.
- **A Mac that signs in to iCloud by itself** (the work-computer build) keeps its CloudKit sign-in token in that Mac’s login keychain. It talks only to Apple’s CloudKit service.
- There is no analytics, advertising or crash reporting.
